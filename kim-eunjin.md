# A Beautiful Mind
In *A Beautiful Mind* (2001), directed by Ron Howard, it tells the story of John Nash who is a mathematician experiencing hallucinations and delusions due to schizophrenia. He struggles to distinguish reality from his mind. The film features *A Kaleidoscope of Mathematics*, an instrumental piece composed by James Horner (1953-2015). The repeating melodies and mysterious atmosphere give the mathematical mind of Nash a complex world. At first, the music seems to help show his intelligence, but as the story goes on, it also questions the boundary between reality and imagination. Instead of directly showing the symptoms of schizophrenia, the music facilitates the process of understanding Nash's experience with psychological confusion.
